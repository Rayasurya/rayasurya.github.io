# Photo Memory Assistant: AI search for a personal photo library, designed around what it does when it is confidently wrong.

- When: September 2026
- Type: Product design take-home, 2 days, working prototype
- Case study with live prototype: https://rayasurya.com/photos-assistant/
- Slides (PDF): https://rayasurya.com/decks/photo-memory-assistant.pdf

**The problem.** A library of about five thousand photos stores file names, dates, places and face tags. People ask for their life: "the one of Rohan at the beach, on the trip in December". The words have nothing in common, so search returns nothing.

**What Raya designed.** It answers in a sentence first, then shows its work. It keeps what it was told (faces, place, time) apart from what it inferred (text in photos, what a photo looks like). A switch next to the search box turns interpretation off, for when the AI hides the thing you wanted. Every photo has a "why this photo" view, with no confidence percentages. Unknown words are named instead of guessed. In the hardest scenario, the persona looks for photos of a father who died in February 2025: that week gets its own row, blurred photos she chooses to reveal, and a menu to stop the app resurfacing them.

**Principle.** Tone is set by the worst moment the product will ever have, not the best.
