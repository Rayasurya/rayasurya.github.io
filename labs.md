# Raya Surya: labs

Tools Raya builds and ships.

- **Any Downloader** (Live on the Chrome Web Store). Detects the media a page has already served to your browser and saves it. https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dpekkpbffjfccnpnajechblepbopihfc
- **Audio Shelf** (Coming soon). A private, local-first book-to-audio app for the Mac.
- **CrawlerBot** (Coming soon). An AI-readability auditor that scores how legible a site is to AI agents.
- **Agent Skills** (Open source). The rulebook Raya uses to direct AI coding agents. https://github.com/Rayasurya/Skills
- **IINA Zoom Controls** (Open source). Keyboard shortcuts that zoom letterboxed video in the IINA player. https://github.com/Rayasurya/iina-zoom-controls
