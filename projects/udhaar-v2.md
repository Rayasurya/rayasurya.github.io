# Mera Udhaar: A payment flow for India's small shops that keeps its promise when the network drops at the counter.

- When: February 2026
- Type: Fintech concept project, 4 weeks
- Case study: https://rayasurya.com/projects/udhaar-v2/

**The problem.** UPI carries over 10 billion payments a month, but networks drop in crowded markets and basements. A failed payment at a busy counter embarrasses the customer and costs the shopkeeper time. Raya recorded an interview with a shopkeeper in Bengaluru about it.

**What Raya designed.** Offline payment that records a signed promise on the phone. An orange screen with a chime means pending but safe, distinct from the green of a settled payment. Pay offline appears only when three local checks pass: device integrity, a Bluetooth beacon at the counter, and an amount below the customer's usual spend. The payment waits, signed in the Android Keystore, and syncs when the network returns; unsent tokens expire after 12 hours.

**What changed.** A first idea, having the shopkeeper scan the customer's code, was scrapped after watching a real counter: it needs the shopkeeper to stop, find and unlock their phone.

**Honesty notes.** A concept. Impact numbers (15% fewer drop-offs, under 10% merchant pushback, 0.5% default risk) are projections, not measurements.
