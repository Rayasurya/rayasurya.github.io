# Preherz: A headset app for rehearsing presentations in realistic rooms, with mentors and an audience.

- When: February 2024
- Type: Earlier work: visionOS concept, 3 weeks, solo
- Case study: https://rayasurya.com/earlier-work/preherz/

**Research.** An online survey of 200 people and 10 interviews: 75% felt moderate to severe anxiety speaking in public, 60% found workshops and clubs out of reach, 80% wanted immersive practice with feedback, 50% preferred AI coaching combined with a human mentor.

**What Raya designed.** Glass panels built on Apple's visionOS design system for the home, connections, conversations and room choice, then full VR rooms to rehearse in and present to an audience.
