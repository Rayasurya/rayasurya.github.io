# Sereni: A journaling app where a daily entry takes a tap and a few spoken words.

- When: January 2024
- Type: Earlier work: mobile app concept, 1 week, solo
- Case study: https://rayasurya.com/earlier-work/sereni/

**What Raya designed.** Voice entries started from a reminder and recorded in the Dynamic Island, an emoji for each day, optional entry guides, progress insights, search by emoji or date, export to PDF and a dark mode. Research was a short survey and two personas.
